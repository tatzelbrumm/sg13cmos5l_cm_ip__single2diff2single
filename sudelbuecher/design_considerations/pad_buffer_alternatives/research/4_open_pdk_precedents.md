# Search 4 — open-PDK precedents (agent report, verbatim)

Bottom line: nothing found is a drop-in precedent for a MOS-only, rail-to-rail, multi-MHz S2D input buffer or class-AB D2S pad driver in sg13cmos5l thick-oxide devices. Closest: (a) Tim Edwards' `sky130_ef_ip__opamp` rail-to-rail driver op amp (3.3 V, 1 940.8 µm² = 36.82 × 52.71 µm by magic_area, 2.37 MHz GBW typ, 122 µA, Vol 0.000 / Voh 3.300 V, full CACE datasheet; topology and ICMR not in the docs); (b) MarvinBrth's Huijsing-style RRIO class-AB op amp on Tiny Tapeout sky26d (3.3 V, 3.10 MHz UGF, A0 99 dB, IQ 259 µA, ICMR 0.05–3.25 V, out 0.05–3.25 V, offset σ 5.0 mV, tile 145 × 226 µm; uses high-poly resistors 14.7k/2×46.9k and three MIM caps 0.66–2.4 pF); (c) YacineSot's fully differential Monticelli class-AB OTA for Chipalooza #2 in sg13cmos5l (1.5 V LV devices, GBW 40–50 MHz at 0.1 pF, MOSCap compensation 3.5 × 21.5 µm, Rc 24 kΩ, CMFB sense resistors 2 × 30 kΩ, area not stated). No published 3.3 V thick-oxide rail-to-rail buffer in sg13g2/sg13cmos5l with area and bandwidth stated.

Other entries:
- vyges capless LDO (sg13cmos5l, 3.3 V): cell 527 × 269 µm, IQ 41.5 µA, loop UGF 1.23 MHz; rhigh resistors and MOM caps `cap_cmomf`/`cap_cmomi` at 1.287 fF/µm².
- orpheus016 programmable instrumentation amp (sg13cmos5l, LV 1.2–1.5 V): two-stage Miller OTA, GBW 2.64 MHz RCX, IQ 18.3 µA; CACE post-layout table is a ready parameter list (A0, F3DB, UGF, PM, GM, GBW, IRN, VOS, IQ, ICMR_MIN/MAX/SPAN, T_DELAY, T_RISE, OVERSHOOT, TS_1PCT, TS_01PCT, SR, CMRR/PSRR at 10 Hz/100 kHz/1 MHz, G_MEAN / GAIN_ERR / GAIN_TC).
- Rodovalho AICSP2022RAIL (sky130 5 V FETs): bulk-driven input pair, composite-transistor stacks, class-AB output, no resistors in the netlists, ~0.8 pF compensation caps; numbers not on GitHub (paper unreachable). GPL-3.0.
- EF_LPOA / EF_HGOA (Chipalooza sky130 2024): targets only — rail-to-rail ICMR, 2.5 / ≥ 12 MHz, 300 µA / 1 mA; no area.
- 2AMLogic gf180-opamp (3.3 V, schematic only): GBW 11.9 MHz worst case, 311 µW, Cc 0.58 pF + Rz 11.2 kΩ. Tsuchiya GF180 op amp: 24.3 MHz, 0.55 mA, compensation resistor as a triode MOS.
- IHP's own open tapeouts (TO_Dec2024/Apr2025/May2025): HBT RF amplifiers only; `sg13g2_IOPadAnalog` is "direct connection, no buffer"; the docs give no cell dimensions.
- Chipalooza slots: tiny 200 × 200, small 500 × 200, large 500 × 415 µm; vyges quotes its harness slot as 537 × 273 µm.
- Open IHP-Open-PDK issue #1252 (buffered analog pad for sg13cmos5l) has no replies.
