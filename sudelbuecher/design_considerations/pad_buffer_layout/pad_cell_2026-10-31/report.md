### 1.5 Pad rules §6.4 (layout rules p. 44) against IHP's ring convention

IHP's convention (`libs.tech/librelane/sg13cmos5l_io/config.tcl`): bond pad `bondpad_70x70` at offset (5, −70) from
the IO cell, i.e. x 5–75, y −70–0, outside the cell. The cell's pad exit is the 70 µm wide strip x 5–75, y 0–3 on
M2 / M3 / M4 / TM1 (`TopLayersTo4`, identical to IOPadAnalog's: 70.00 µm on each at y = 1.5).
- **Pad.jR** (no devices under the pad): the pad is at y < 0; the cell has no shape below y 0. Pass, as IHP.
- **Pad.d1R** (pad to Activ ≥ 11.2 µm, recommended): the lowest Activ is at y 6.0 (Clamp_N frame ring), 6.0 µm from
  the pad. Not met, identically in IOPadAnalog (Activ from y 6.0). The deck's `Pad.d1R` reports 0, because there is no
  dfpad / Passiv in the row (the deck tests pad rules only within dfpad).
- **Pad.eR / Pad.fR** (exit ≥ 7 µm wide and long): the TM1 exit is 70 µm wide but 3 µm long (y 0–3); from y 3 the pad
  continues as M2 drain straps of 0.61 µm. The same as IOPadAnalog. Recommended rules; IHP's own cells don't meet fR.
