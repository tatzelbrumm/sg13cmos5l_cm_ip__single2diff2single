# Real bias for `d2s_mpdda`

2026-10-05. The ideal reference currents of `d2s_bias_lp` replaced by transistors, in four variants:

1. `d2s_bias_in`: the reference current comes from an external PMOS source.
2. `d2s_bias_out`: the reference current goes into an external NMOS sink.
3. `d2s_bias_oa`: self-contained, without resistors or BJTs.
4. `d2s_bias_bg`: self-contained bandgap with vertical PNPs.

Netlists are in `sim/d2s_bias_ref.spice`. Every number below comes from `sim/run_bias.py` and is in
`sim/results_bias.txt`. Corners are hv MOS tt/ss/ff/sf/fs, rhigh typ/wcs/bcs, pnpMPA typ/wcs/bcs,
−40/27/125 °C and 3.0/3.3/3.6 V.

## Common structure

All variants keep `d2s_bias_lp`'s six diodes (`d2s_bias_diodes`), and with them the bias voltages the
driver sees. Only the currents into the diodes are generated differently.

| line | current | diode | biases |
|---|---|---|---|
| vbp | 5 µA out of | BP 5/6 | unit tails Ta, Tb |
| vbn | 5 µA into | BN 6/6 | fold sinks SX, SY |
| vbpc | 2 µA out of | BPC 1/4 | mirror cascodes PCL, PCR |
| vbnc | 2 µA into | BNC 1/8 | fold cascodes CX, CY |
| vabp | 5 µA out of | RP2 on RP1 | ABP, FPL (class-AB loop, P side) |
| vabn | 5 µA into | RN2 on RN1 | ABN, FNL (class-AB loop, N side) |

**Rail referencing.** Every gate line is generated by a diode on the rail of the devices it biases:

- PMOS lines (vbp, vbpc, vabp) come from PMOS diodes on vdd.
- NMOS lines come from NMOS diodes on vss.

Bias crosses from one block to another only as currents, never as voltages.

**Supply partition.** The front end and the bias run on the slot's analog supply, vdd/vss (vdd_3v3,
vss_3v3). The output devices OP/ON get their own rails, vddo/vsso, now ports of every driver and bias
cell (`vdd vss vddo vsso ...`). The slot has no separate IO supply, so vddo comes from vdd_3v3.
Whether vsso connects to vssio or to vss_3v3 is still open.

- The class-AB replicas RP1 and RN1 sit on vddo/vsso, not on the bias rails. A voltage between vdd
  and vddo adds directly to the translinear loop V_SG(OP) + V_SG(ABP) = V_SG(RP1) + V_SG(RP2).
- RP2's n-well stays on vdd, like ABP's.
- RN1, like ON in its clamp frame, needs a local substrate tap ring on vsso.
- With vddo = vdd and vsso = vss, the netlists also fit a single-supply driver.

| replicas RP1/RN1 | I_Q at vddo −50 mV | nominal | vddo +50 mV | vsso −50 mV | vsso +50 mV |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| on vddo/vsso (as built) | 211.0 µA | 212.0 | 213.0 | 213.3 | 210.8 |
| on vdd/vss | 155.3 | 212.0 | 298.2 | 298.0 | 156.6 |

So in layout, RP1's source has to be taken from OP's source rail, and RN1's from ON's (Kelvin),
not from the nearest vdd or vss.

## Variants 1 and 2: mirrors from an external reference

- **`d2s_bias_in`** has a separate input diode NI (a copy of BN), so iref is not one of the driver's
  gate lines.
  - NI drives three NMOS sinks, out of BP, BPC and RP2.
  - BP drives three PMOS sources, into BN, BNC and RN2.
- **`d2s_bias_out`** is the mirror image, with input diode PI (a copy of BP).
- Each is 7 devices and about 190 µm² of gate area. The reference is 5 µA nominal. All currents scale
  with it, and NI/PI can be resized for another harness current; the harness's `ibias` value is not
  known yet.

## Variant 3: `d2s_bias_oa`, no resistor, no BJT

This is the Oguey–Aebischer core (H. J. Oguey, D. Aebischer, IEEE JSSC 32(7), 1997), re-derived for
sg13_hv. The project's `macros/OgueyAebischerBias` is a direct sky130 port and was never re-sized. The
2026-09-04 session measured 47 nA instead of 200 nA, 27.5 %/V supply sensitivity and very large
mismatch.

**How it works.**

- M11 (8 I0) and M10 (twice M11's size, I0) share their gate and run in weak inversion. That fixes the
  source of M10 at vres = U_T ln 16 = 72 mV, simulated 72.3 mV.
- The composite pair shares gate vbr and has one size (3.4 × 30 µm): MCS is a diode at 2 I0, MCL a
  triode device at I0. MCL drops exactly vres at only one inversion level.
- Hence I0 = const · 2nμC_ox·U_T²·(W/L)_C, independent of V_T.
- I0 = 0.25 µA, and the output is 20 I0 = 5 µA into variant 1's mirror tree.

**What the redesign changed.**

- M10 and M11 are in weak inversion. In moderate inversion, both sides of the loop equation move with
  current, the current goes roughly as the square of the composite width, and mismatch is amplified.
- MC10 cascodes M10. Its channel-length modulation at L = 1 µm was the main cause of the original
  supply sensitivity.
- The PMOS mirror has stacked-diode cascodes. Line sensitivity fell from 2.2 to about 1 %/V, most of
  it now from the mirror tree.
- **Start-up:**
  - MS1 is a weak, always-on pull-up (0.5/100, about 0.46 µA).
  - MS3 pulls the cascode line vpc down while ks is high.
  - MS2 senses vbr, which is 0 V when the core is off and about 0.9 V when it runs. Sensing vnc
    (0.54 V) instead left 35 nA of start-up current in the core.
  - When running, the start-up leaks 4 fA.

**Inherent limit: temperature.** The current goes as μ·T², close to T^1.3 here. Relative to tt
27 °C that is −35 % at −40 °C and +33 % at 125 °C; −39 % at ss/3.0 V/−40 °C and +42 % at
ff/3.6 V/125 °C. Compensating it without a resistor or BJT would need a second, CTAT, resistorless
branch; I didn't attempt that.

## Variant 4: `d2s_bias_bg`, bandgap with pnpMPA

This is a current-mode (Banba-type) bandgap with no op-amp. The PMOS mirror makes the two branch
currents equal, and the matched NMOS pair NA/NB forces ea = eb. Each branch then carries

  I = V_BE1/R1 + U_T ln 8/R0, with Q2 = 8 × Q1 (4 µm² each) and R1, R0 in rhigh.

**Why rhigh.** Its TC is −0.22 %/K (rppd's is +0.017 %/K). That nearly cancels V_BE's −1.77 mV/K by
itself: V_BE/R_rhigh alone drifts only −0.03 %/K at 27 °C. So the PTAT share is small, and the NMOS
pair's offset hardly matters. A by-product: the DDA units are degenerated by the same rhigh, so their
I·R product, which sets the input linear range, does not move with rhigh's process spread.

**False operating point.** With R1A = R1B, the core can rest with both PNPs off, because the loop gain
is then exactly 1. It did so at −40 °C. R1A is therefore 10 % longer than R1B. With the PNPs off the
loop gain is now above 1 and the core runs up until they conduct. R0 (0.5 × 179.5 µm) is trimmed for
the flattest current with that asymmetry.

**Headroom.** The stacked cascodes need low V_SG. The PMOS are 40/1 in moderate inversion, which keeps
NB saturated at ss/3.0 V/−40 °C (V_DS 0.32 V against V_DSsat 0.12 V).

**Start-up** works as in variant 3, sensing the NMOS gate line g.

A few notes on the table:

- The Oguey–Aebischer spread is dominated by the M10/M11 and composite pairs; halving it would take
  about four times their area (~800 µm² more). The bandgap's spread is mostly its 2:1 output mirror.
- pnpMPA has no mismatch model, so the bandgap's mismatch figure leaves the PNPs out.
- Start-up was checked from 0 V with every node at 0, over tt, ss/3.0 V/−40 °C, ff/3.6 V/125 °C,
  sf −40 °C, fs 125 °C and tt at −40/125 °C. Every case ends at the DC operating point, and t90 follows
  the ramp.

## Results, driver

`d2s_mpdda` runs with OP/ON on vddo/vsso, a 1 kΩ ∥ 100 pF load, closed loop. At tt 27 °C every
variant gives I_Q 212 µA (variant 2: 217), T0 78.9 dB, f_c 1.37 MHz, PM 72.4° and offset 0.06 mV,
the same as the ideal fixture.

Over PVT:

- **Variants 1 and 2:** variant 1 tracks the ideal fixture within 1 %; variant 2 runs 2.6–3.5 % higher.
- **Bandgap:** within −2.3…+0.5 % of the ideal fixture, except for the rhigh corners (I_Q 171 / 268 µA).
- **Oguey–Aebischer:**
  - I_Q runs from 145 µA at −40 °C to 276 µA at 125 °C, and 128–305 µA at the supply/process
    extremes.
  - The hot end costs loop gain and offset: at tt 125 °C, T0 is 47.8 dB (ideal fixture 56.3) and the
    offset 2.7 mV (0.8). At ff/3.6 V/125 °C it is 41.8 dB and 5.3 mV.

**Supply rejection**, |vout/v_rail| in dB (signal gain is −6 dB):

| rail | bias | 10 Hz | 1 kHz | 100 kHz | 1 MHz | 10 MHz |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| vdd | ideal / in / oa / bg | −85 / −84 / −84 / −84 | −85 / −84 / −84 / −84 | −65 / −66 / −68 / −67 | −46 / −48 / −47 / −51 | −45 / −45 / −45 / −41 |
| vss | ideal / in / oa / bg | −73 / −72 / −72 / −72 | −73 / −72 / −72 / −72 | −72 / −72 / −71 / −70 | −60 / −61 / −69 / −45 | −47 / −47 / −47 / −40 |
| vddo | all | −80…−81 | −71 | −31 | −12 | −14 |
| vsso | all | −71…−72 | −68 | −32 | −12.5 | −13.5 |

The bias variants differ by a few dB. The one exception is the bandgap from vss at 1 MHz: its
self-biased loop peaks there, and damping it would take about 10 pF of rail-referenced MOS capacitance
(about 2000 µm²).

**The limit is the output-stage rails.** The Miller capacitors tie the gates a and b to vout, so ripple
on OP's or ON's source rail is rejected only by the loop gain:

- −31 dB at 100 kHz and −12 dB at 1 MHz.
- This doesn't depend on the bias. It is a property of the Miller-compensated output stage and of
  which rail it sits on.
- Whatever noise sits on vddo and on vsso (vssio or vss_3v3) reaches the output at those levels.
- Options:
  - a quiet output-stage rail;
  - cascode (Ahuja) compensation, which unties the gates from vout. The first note chose plain Miller
    for phase margin; this would be a reason to revisit that.

ON's bulk in these simulations is vsso, its frame's local tap ring. With the bulk on the front end's
vss instead, the vss path drops to −15 dB at 1 MHz, an artefact of the model.

## Open points

- The harness `ibias` current and direction are not known. Variants 1/2 assume 5 µA and scale with it.
- No enable pin. The slot's supply switch powers the block down, and both self-contained cores start
  from ramps of 1–100 µs. A `disable` (as in the existing macro) is a small addition if needed.
- Variant 3's temperature coefficient and variant 4's rhigh spread both go straight into I_Q. Which
  matters more depends on the specification (supply current budget, crossover distortion at
  temperature).
