JanteStroemSpejl contact-to-port map
====================================

Visible-contact naming convention
---------------------------------
DL01..DL11 : left outer yellow diffusion contacts, top to bottom
DR01..DR11 : right outer yellow diffusion contacts, top to bottom
S          : bottom-center yellow diffusion contact
GTL, GBL   : upper/lower gray contact on left poly edge
GTR, GBR   : upper/lower gray contact on right poly edge

Electrical assignment
---------------------
S -> common source port S.  The central vertical yellow diffusion is the
same source net.

GTL, GBL, GTR, GBR -> GREF.  They are interpreted as poly contacts
strapped together by the white vertical metal.

DL06 and DR06 -> GREF.  Row 6 lies under the white horizontal metal arm,
so its two drains are tied to the gate/reference node.  XJL06 and XJR06
are therefore the diode-connected reference pair.

DL01..DL05, DL07..DL11 -> ten left drain terminals.
DR01..DR05, DR07..DR11 -> ten right drain terminals.

RAW port version
----------------
Keep all twenty non-reference drain contacts independent.  This is the
closest electrical representation of what is visibly present.

MIRROR10 wrapper
----------------
Pair same-row contacts externally:
  OUT01 = DL01 + DR01
  OUT02 = DL02 + DR02
  OUT03 = DL03 + DR03
  OUT04 = DL04 + DR04
  OUT05 = DL05 + DR05
  OUT06 = DL07 + DR07
  OUT07 = DL08 + DR08
  OUT08 = DL09 + DR09
  OUT09 = DL10 + DR10
  OUT10 = DL11 + DR11

This pairing is not drawn as metal in the artwork; it is a proposed port
assignment that converts the symmetric layout into a ten-output current
mirror.  The physical artwork itself only forces the row-6 reference
connection and the common source/gate nets under this interpretation.

Body
----
The body/well connection is not explicitly encoded by a visible contact
in the artwork.  Keep B as a separate subcircuit terminal in JANTE_RAW,
and normally connect B=S in the wrapper/testbench for an NMOS mirror.
